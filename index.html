What this is

We went through Brainly as someone who just installed it, then rebuilt the parts we think leave money on the table.

We power more than 30,000 subscription apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market. We installed Brainly, walked from the first screen through sign-up to the plan picker behind “Maybe later”, read your App Store listing and brainly.com, and looked at the searches your Apple Ads show up on. Then we looked for gaps between what you already have and what a new user gets to see.

So we built two flows on your own screens. Flow 1 personalizes everything after four onboarding answers, including whether a student or a parent is holding the phone: your listing and your website now speak to parents first, the app still speaks to students only. Flow 2 personalizes the paywall by the Apple Ads keyword that brought the install, before they answer anything. They stack: the keyword decides what the paywall leads with, the answers sharpen everything after it.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen uses your wordmark, your lime button, your condensed paywall headline, your purple subject chips, your gold plan card and your mascot. Only the order and the words around them change.

Your ads found homework and tutor searches. Your paywall never knew.
